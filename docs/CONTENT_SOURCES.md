# AI Training Content Research Notes

The pilot curriculum uses original wording and DASI-specific fictional scenarios. It does not copy another course. The learning sequence and risk vocabulary are informed by the following public, authoritative materials.

## Primary references

1. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0** — organizes AI risk work around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage and emphasizes context, impact, monitoring, and human accountability.  
   https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
2. **NIST AI 600-1, Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile** — identifies generative-AI risks including confabulation, data privacy, information integrity, human-AI configuration, intellectual property, and information security. This pilot uses the term “confabulation” alongside the familiar term “hallucination.”  
   https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
3. **CISA Artificial Intelligence guidance hub** — reinforces secure and responsible adoption, protection of sensitive information, and risk-aware use of AI systems.  
   https://www.cisa.gov/ai
4. **OECD AI Principles** — emphasizes human-centered values, transparency, robustness, security, safety, and accountability.  
   https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles
5. **FAA Roadmap for Artificial Intelligence Safety Assurance** — provides aviation context for maintaining appropriate assurance and qualified human oversight around safety-relevant AI use.  
   https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/step/roadmap_for_AI_safety_assurance

## How the research maps to this pilot

| Learning element | Research basis | DASI application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI is prediction, not judgment | AI literacy foundations; NIST human-AI configuration | Learners do not treat fluent output as qualified sourcing or airworthiness judgment. |
| Match controls to impact | NIST Map/Measure/Manage | Low-risk drafts receive lighter checks than safety-relevant, regulated, or high-value decisions. |
| Confabulation and information integrity | NIST GAI Profile | Learners verify part, trace, price, stock, and lead-time claims against source records. |
| Data minimization and approved tools | NIST privacy/security risks; CISA secure adoption | Customer, supplier, RFQ, pricing, trace, contract, personal, and controlled information stays in approved workflows. |
| Human accountability | NIST, OECD, and FAA principles | A qualified DASI owner reviews important output and remains responsible for the decision. |
| Active retrieval and scenario practice | Common evidence-based instructional design | Two preflight knowledge checks precede an acted AOG sourcing scenario and coached decisions. |

## Content governance

- Organization-specific statements are labeled as DASI procedures or placeholders and must be reviewed against actual DASI policies before production release.
- Legal, regulatory, export-control, airworthiness, and contractual claims require review by the appropriate DASI subject-matter owner.
- Source links and course content should be reviewed at each training-version release.
- Fictional customer names, phone numbers, RFQ numbers, and sourcing details are used in the scenario.
